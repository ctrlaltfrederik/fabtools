# Talks

Slide decks (as delivered, exported to PDF) from conference and community talks on
Microsoft Fabric capacity and cost.

## [The ultimate decision guide: capacity strategies to optimise cost and compute](fabcon-2026-capacities-decision-guide/)

FabCon Europe, Barcelona, September 2026 (session T44). The three tools in this repo —
Fabric Capacity Advisor, the licensing crossover, and the capacity levers — are the
take-home artifacts for this talk.

→ [PDF](fabcon-2026-capacities-decision-guide/the-ultimate-decision-guide.pdf)

## [We're at capacity, now what?](fabcon-2025-were-at-capacity-now-what/)

FabCon Europe, Vienna, September 2025 (session TH32). Covers Fabric capacity basics —
background vs. interactive operations, bursting, smoothing — and what to do when a
capacity is maxed out.
